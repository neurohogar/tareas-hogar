# Package: block-reschedule

Second feature of epic E6 of `docs/v3/plan.md` («Low-capacity days and return without debt»): a block rescheduling, by hand from the **⋮** menu of **Pendientes** and with a preview of names, that lets the person come back after an absence without the overdue recurring tasks («Tocaba …») piling up as debt: all of them at once, as a **Reanudar** at the moment of confirming, with a 5-second **Deshacer** notice. Overdue one-off tasks are left out and keep their «Tocaba …». The «esencial» mark and the minimal mode were already done in `minimal-mode`.

## Specs

- **S1** (§5.11): «**Block rescheduling** («Reprogramar vencidas») lets the person come back after an absence without the overdue recurring tasks piling up as debt. It is done by hand from the **⋮** menu of **Pendientes** (§6.1): the app never offers it or does it by itself.»
- **S2** (§5.11): «**Which tasks**: every **recurring** task that is `overdue` at that moment and in view (§6.1), with no choice: a task cannot be left out. One-off tasks are not rescheduled even if they are overdue and keep their «Tocaba …». Paused tasks (by hand or by the minimal mode) have no status (§5.8), a postponed task is `waiting` (§5.9) and `pending` and `waiting` tasks carry no debt: all of these stay untouched. With the minimal mode on, it works the same on the overdue recurring tasks in view, which are the essential ones (§5.8, §6.1).»
- **S3** (§5.11): «**What it does**: it is a **Reanudar** (§5.8) of each of those tasks, with `reanudadaEn` = the moment of confirming. Each one is evaluated as newly created at that moment: relative tasks are `pending` at once; calendar tasks are `pending` if today is one of their days and `waiting` otherwise; with a schedule, §5.8 applies (an occurrence whose window closed before `reanudadaEn` does not count as missed). The «Tocaba …» of what was missed goes away and does not come back later: the filter by `reanudadaEn` is permanent (§5.8). Example: a relative task of every 2 days, last completed `2026-07-10T09:00:00`, is `overdue` («Tocaba hace 4 días») at `2026-07-16T10:00:00`; confirmed at that moment, it is `pending`, with `reanudadaEn` = `2026-07-16T10:00:00`.»
- **S4** (§5.11): «**Only `reanudadaEn`**: it writes nothing else. Title, emoji, note, steps and `pasoActual`, minimal version, `essential`, schedule, recurrence, `creadaEn`, `pausada` and `pospuesta` do not change. It adds no completion and deletes none: the **Historial** and «Última vez» do not change (§4.4).»
- **S5** (§5.11): «**Undo**: the notice of §5.4 has **Deshacer**. Undoing writes back, for each rescheduled task that still exists, the value of `reanudadaEn` that the rescheduling replaced (or removes the field if the task had none), and nothing else. Completions made after the rescheduling stay; a task deleted in between is not revived (§7).»
- **S6** (§5.11): «**No summary**: neither the preview, nor confirming, nor undoing shows a count of how many tasks were rescheduled, a message about what had piled up, or congratulations.»
- **S7** (§5.4): «The notice of a block rescheduling (§5.11) is «Tareas reprogramadas», with **Deshacer**, and shares this single slot: it lasts **5 seconds** from the moment of confirming (shown while confirmation ≤ now < confirmation + 5 s), and either notice replaces the other. The replaced one loses its **Deshacer**: completing a task within those 5 seconds replaces the rescheduling notice and the rescheduling can no longer be undone from the notice. The same notice stays visible when changing screens, until it expires. **Deshacer** in it undoes the rescheduling (§5.11) and closes the notice.»
- **S8** (§6.1): «**Menu and minimal mode**: the header of **Pendientes** has a small **⋮** button, with the accessible name «Menú», that opens a menu with two items: **Modo mínimo**, which shows whether the mode is on (a check mark when on), and **Reprogramar vencidas** (below). Tapping **Modo mínimo** acts at that moment, without confirmation: it turns the mode on or off (§5.8) and closes the menu. The menu has no other items; "Completadas" and "Pausadas" stay where they are. While the mode is on, above the list there is a line with the text «Modo mínimo» in secondary text, without color, icon or counter. It is only text (tapping it does nothing) and it is shown even if the list is empty (with the mode on and no essential task, the list is empty with that line). Turning the mode on or off does not show any other message (§5.8).»
- **S9** (§6.1): «**Reprogramar vencidas** (§5.11): the second item of the **⋮** menu, below **Modo mínimo**. It is **disabled** (shown dimmed, tapping it does nothing and the menu stays open) whenever there is no overdue recurring task in view, including when the only overdue tasks are one-off ones or, with the minimal mode on, non-essential ones; the menu always looks the same. Enabled, tapping it closes the menu and opens a **preview**: a title «Reprogramar vencidas», a line «Vuelven como recién creadas, desde ahora.», the list of the tasks that will be rescheduled, and the buttons **Reprogramar** and **Cancelar**. Each row shows only the emoji and the title (§4.1), in the order of this section, with no «Tocaba …», no date, no checks and no count. The preview follows the data and the clock while it is open: a task that another device completed, deleted or paused, or that stops being an overdue recurring task, leaves it, and one that becomes one enters it. **Reprogramar** acts on all the tasks that are in the preview at that moment, without further confirmation, closes the preview and shows the notice of §5.4. If the preview is left with no task, **Reprogramar** is disabled and it can only be closed; **Cancelar** closes it and changes nothing.»
- **S10** (§7): «**Block rescheduling** (§5.11): the `reanudadaEn` of all the rescheduled tasks is written in a single `writeBatch`, with the moment of confirming as "now", and works offline like the rest of the app. Undoing is likewise a single `writeBatch`. The unit of conflict stays per field (`reanudadaEn`); a deleted task is not revived by the undo.»

## Decisions

- D1 (2026-11-23, analyst, what rescheduling does to the debt): a rescheduled task (always recurring, D11) stops being `overdue` and carries no «Tocaba …» of what was missed; it does not come back as overdue later because of the time before the rescheduling (with D10 this is the permanent `reanudadaEn` filter of §5.8). Because the epic is «return without debt» and the plan refutes it with «a way out that is felt as debt»; a plain postponement (§5.9) does not move the occurrences and would bring them back overdue.
- D2 (2026-11-23, analyst, completions and history): rescheduling adds no completion and deletes none; the **Historial** and «Última vez» do not change. Because completions are append-only and record what was done (§4.4), and rescheduling is not doing the task.
- D3 (2026-11-23, analyst, tasks out of the rescheduling): only recurring tasks that are `overdue` at that moment and in view are rescheduled (all of them, D12). One-off tasks are out even if overdue (D11) and keep their «Tocaba …»; paused tasks (by hand or by the minimal mode) have no status (§5.8); a postponed task is `waiting` (§5.9); `pending` and `waiting` tasks carry no debt text. All of these stay untouched. Because D11 limits the rescheduling to recurring tasks and the rest carry no debt to take away.
- D4 (2026-11-23, analyst, other fields): rescheduling writes only `reanudadaEn` (D10) and does not change the title, emoji, note, steps or `pasoActual`, minimal version, `essential`, schedule, recurrence, `creadaEn`, `pausada` or `pospuesta`. Because the rescheduling is a **Reanudar** (D10), §4.1 says those fields do not change when the task is resumed, and the tasks it acts on are not paused (D3), so there is no `pausada` to remove.
- D5 (2026-11-23, analyst, sync): the rescheduling of all the selected tasks is written in a single `writeBatch`, with the moment of confirming as "now", and works offline like the rest of the app; undo is likewise a single `writeBatch`. Because turning the minimal mode off, also an action on many tasks, is written that way (§7) and the unit of conflict stays per field.
- D6 (2026-11-23, analyst, preview kept up to date): while the preview is open it follows the data and the clock (a task that another device completed, deleted, paused or that stops being an overdue recurring task leaves the preview; one that becomes one enters it), and confirming acts on all the tasks that are still in it at that moment (D12: there is nothing to select). Because the action notice of §6.2 already follows that rule ("kept up to date while it is open ... evaluated again on tapping").
- D7 (2026-11-23, analyst, what undo restores): undo writes back, for each rescheduled task that still exists, the value of `reanudadaEn` that the rescheduling replaced (or removes the field if the task had none), and nothing else: completions made after the rescheduling stay; a task deleted in between is not revived. Because deletion wins over an edit (§7) and completions are never lost (§4.4).
- D8 (2026-11-23, analyst, no summary): neither the preview, nor confirming, nor undoing shows a count of how many tasks were rescheduled, a message about what had piled up, or congratulations (the preview lists names only, D14). Because the plan's Avoid line forbids «a summary of what piled up», and §5.8 (**No summary**) and §5.4 (no congratulations) already follow it.
- D9 (2026-11-23, analyst, minimal mode on): with the mode on, the rescheduling works the same on the overdue recurring tasks in view (the essential ones, §6.1). Because the mode «changes nothing» of essential tasks (§5.8), the non-essential recurring ones are paused by the mode (no status, D3) and one-off tasks are out anyway (D11).

- D10 (2026-11-23, user, Q1 when the rescheduled tasks come back): «Ahora, como recién creadas (Recomendada)» (translation: «Now, as if newly created (Recommended)»). Option description: like turning off Modo mínimo (a Reanudar at the moment of confirming); reuses §5.8 as it is; every interval (relative) task becomes `pending` at once: the «Tocaba» goes away, but the list does not get shorter right away.
- D11 (2026-11-23, user, Q2 overdue one-off tasks): «No se reprograman» (translation: «They are not rescheduled»). Option description: one-off tasks keep their «Tocaba»; the rule is simpler, but some debt stays in view. Only the recurring overdue tasks are rescheduled.
- D12 (2026-11-23, user, Q3 which tasks go in): «Todas las vencidas, sin elegir» (translation: «All the overdue ones, with no choice»). Option description: one gesture; a task that should keep its «Tocaba» cannot be left out. The question's note: with D11, one-off overdue tasks are already out; this applies to the recurring overdue ones.
- D13 (2026-11-23, user, Q4 entry point): «Solo a mano, en el menú ⋮ (Recomendada)» (translation: «Only by hand, in the ⋮ menu (Recommended)»). Option description: a new item in the ⋮ menu of Pendientes next to Modo mínimo; the app never offers it.
- D14 (2026-11-23, user, Q5 preview rows): «Solo el nombre (Recomendada)» (translation: «Only the name (Recommended)»). Option description: emoji and title; no «Tocaba», so nothing reminds the person of what piled up. The question noted there are no per-task checks (D12).
- D15 (2026-11-23, user, Q6 undo form): «Aviso con Deshacer por 5 segundos (Recomendada)» (translation: «Notice with Deshacer for 5 seconds (Recommended)»). Option description: in the same single notice slot as §5.4; completing a task within those 5 seconds replaces the notice and the undo is lost.
- D16 (2026-11-23, user, Q7 no overdue tasks): «El ítem se ve deshabilitado (Recomendada)» (translation: «The item is shown disabled (Recommended)»). Option description: the menu always looks the same, which is predictable.
- D17 (2026-11-23, analyst, the ⋮ menu gets a second item): the text of §6.1 that says the menu has a single item, **Modo mínimo**, and «no other items» changes to include the rescheduling item (D13). Because the user chose that entry point; the rest of the menu (acting and closing) stays as it is.
- D18 (2026-11-23, analyst, when the item is disabled): the item is disabled whenever there is no overdue recurring task in view, including when the only overdue tasks are one-off ones or, with the minimal mode on, non-essential ones. Because D16 disables it when there is nothing to reschedule, and by D3, D9 and D11 those tasks are not rescheduled.
- D19 (2026-11-23, analyst, preview left empty): if the preview is left with no task while open (D6), it cannot be confirmed (only closed), like the item of D18. Because confirming would do nothing and D16 already chose a disabled control over a hidden one.
- D20 (2026-11-23, analyst, notice slot): the rescheduling notice and the completion notice share the single slot of §5.4: either one replaces the other, the replaced one loses its **Deshacer**, and it stays visible across screens until it expires. Because D15 puts it in that slot and §5.4 already defines its behavior.
