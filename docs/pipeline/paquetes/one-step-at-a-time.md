# Package: one-step-at-a-time

Second feature of epic E3 of `docs/v3/plan.md` («Primer paso a la vista»): optional steps for long tasks, shown one at a time. The first step of the `first-step` feature becomes step 1 of a list that is written in the panel; the row shows a single step («Primer paso: …» or «Paso: …») and, if the task has two steps or more, a «Hecho» button that moves to the next one (on the last one, it completes the task) and a «Paso anterior» button to go back. A one-step task looks as it does today, with no buttons. Completing the task returns it to step 1.

## Specs

- **S1** (§4.1): «| `pasos` | string[] | no | Steps 2 onward, in order (see **Steps**, below). Any kind of task and any effort. |»
- **S2** (§4.1): «| `pasoActual` | integer ≥ 1 | no | Number of the step the task is on (see **Steps**). Without the field, it is on step 1. |»
- **S3** (§4.1): «**First step**: a single line, with no line breaks and no maximum length. On save, spaces at the start and at the end are trimmed. Empty or only spaces means **no first step** (the field is not saved). A first step is never invalid: it never rejects the creation or the edit. The first step is step 1 of the **Steps** list (below). In a one-step task it has no state: completing the task, postponing it, pausing it, resuming it, undoing and changing the kind or the mode do not erase it or change it, and a recurring task that comes back shows the same first step. In a one-step task there is no way to mark it as done and no progress indicator; in a task of two steps or more there is (see **Steps**). A task saved without the field has no first step (there is no migration). It does not change the status (§5), the order of the list, the filters or **Solo rápidas**, and the task name (below) is still emoji and title. It is not shown in the **Historial**, in the undo notice, in Anotaciones or in the copied text.»
- **S4** (§4.1): «**Steps**: a task can have, besides the first step, more steps, optional and for any kind of task and any effort. The first step is step 1 and `pasos` stores steps 2, 3… in the written order; a task saved without `pasos` has no more steps (there is no migration). A task has **a single step** if it has a first step and no `pasos`, and **two steps or more** if it has a first step and at least one step in `pasos`.»
- **S5** (§4.1): «- **Text**: each step follows the first step rule: a single line, with no line breaks and no maximum length, spaces at the start and at the end are trimmed on save, and an empty step or one with only spaces is discarded on save, without error. The steps below move up: if the first step is left empty and step 2 is not, step 2 becomes the first step. A step is never invalid and never rejects the creation or the edit, and there is no maximum number of steps.»
- **S6** (§4.1): «- **Progress**: `pasoActual` is the number of the step the task is on. «Hecho» (§6.1) takes it to the next step and «Paso anterior» (§6.1) to the previous one; neither goes past the first or the last. Editing the steps does not change that number: if fewer steps remain than `pasoActual`, the task is on the last one; if a single step remains, it is a one-step task. The app does not change the progress by itself.»
- **S7** (§4.1): «- **Complete**: tapping «Hecho» on the last step of a task of two steps or more does exactly what tapping the checkbox of that uncompleted task does (it adds the same completion, opens the same notice of §5.4 and, if the checkbox is disabled by its window, §5.5, the «Hecho» of the last step is also disabled; that of the other steps is not). Completing the task, by the checkbox or by «Hecho», returns `pasoActual` to step 1, so a recurring task that comes back starts from the beginning. The completion stores in `pasoAlCompletar` the number of the step the task was on.»
- **S8** (§4.1): «- **Undo**: deleting the most recent completion of the task (notice, **Historial** or checkbox, §5.4) returns `pasoActual` to the value of its `pasoAlCompletar`, capped at the last step; for a task completed with «Hecho» on the last step, it stays on the last step. There is no undo notice for «Hecho» on a step that is not the last: it is corrected with «Paso anterior».»
- **S9** (§4.1): «- **No other effects**: postponing, removing the postponement, pausing, resuming and changing the kind or the mode do not erase or change the steps or `pasoActual`. The steps and their progress do not change the status (§5), the order of the list, the filters or **Solo rápidas**, and are not shown in the **Historial**, in the undo notice, in Anotaciones or in the copied text. The row never shows the whole list (§6.1).»
- **S10** (§4.4): «| `pasoAlCompletar` | integer ≥ 1 | no | Step the task was on when it was completed (**Steps**, §4.1). Only in tasks of two steps or more. |»
- **S11** (§5.4): «If the deleted completion is the most recent one of a task of two steps or more, `pasoActual` returns to the `pasoAlCompletar` of that completion (§4.1, **Steps**).»
- **S12** (§6.1): «If the task is `pending` or `overdue` and has a first step (§4.1), on its own line below the title, the step the task is on: «Primer paso: » followed by the text if it is on step 1 (for example «Primer paso: Separar la ropa blanca»), or «Paso: » followed by the text if it is on another one (for example «Paso: Poner el lavarropas»), without number or total; it never shows the whole list. It is shown in full, on several lines if needed, without cutting, in the same size and secondary text color as the effort and the schedule, without emphasis or its own color. It is not shown in `waiting` rows (nor the postponed or completed ones) or in the paused ones. It is decided with the status of each moment: the line disappears when the task becomes `waiting` (on completing it, postponing it or because of its schedule) or is paused, and comes back by itself when it becomes `pending` or `overdue` again. An `overdue` task with the checkbox disabled until its window opens (§5.5) shows it. Tapping the step text is tapping the row (it opens the panel); in a one-step task the line is not edited from the row and has no buttons.»
- **S13** (§6.1): «In a task of two steps or more (§4.1, **Steps**), on the step line, and therefore only when the line is shown, two small text buttons, without their own color: **Paso anterior** (before **Hecho**; not shown on step 1) and **Hecho**. **Hecho** moves to the next step; on the last step it completes the task like the checkbox (§5.4: same completion, same notice «Completaste «…»» with **Deshacer**, and `pasoActual` returns to step 1), and it is disabled if the checkbox is (§5.5). **Paso anterior** returns to the previous step. Tapping them does not open the panel, does not go through **Unsaved changes** and does not open an undo notice, except the «Hecho» of the last step.»
- **S14** (§6.2): «- **More steps**: below the first step, each more step is a one-line field with the label «Paso 2», «Paso 3»…, with a **Quitar** button each, and at the end an **Agregar paso** button that adds an empty field, like **Agregar horario**. Enter in those fields saves. The steps go in the written order, without reordering; on save, the empty ones are discarded and the ones below move up (§4.1, **Steps**). Adding, removing or changing a step counts for **Unsaved changes**; typing and going back to the original value does not count, nor do the spaces that **Guardar** trims or an empty step that **Guardar** discards. The panel does not show or choose the step the task is on: that is corrected from the row.»
- **S15** (§6.2): «- **Tarea**: at the top, "Pausada" (if applicable), "Pospuesta hasta HH:MM" (or "hasta mañana HH:MM") and **Última vez**; then the title with the emoji beside it (the emoji button next to the Título field, with **Quitar emoji** below); the first step and the more steps; the note; the effort; the labels.»
- **S16** (§7): «Tasks and labels: **the last change wins, per field** (`emoji`, `nota` and `primerPaso` are independent fields, like `titulo`; `horario` is a single field: its windows, with `desde` and `hasta`, are written together; pausing and resuming write `pausada` and `reanudadaEn` together; `pospuesta` is a single field; `pasos` is a single field that is replaced whole, like `etiquetas`, and `pasoActual` is another independent field: a «Hecho» in the row does not lose what another device edited in the panel; two «Hecho» offline on the same step leave the task on the next step, not two further along). Edits are partial updates (`update` / `set` with `merge`), never replacements of the whole document. An edit writes only the fields that changed.»

## Decisions

- D1 (2026-11-13, analyst, scope): this feature covers the «optional steps for long tasks, shown one at a time» of E3 and closes the epic. It does not change the status (§5), the order of the list (§6.1), the filters or **Solo rápidas**, and the task name (§4.1) is still emoji and title. Because D1 of `first-step` left the steps themselves for this feature, and the plan asks that E3 not touch the status rules (E7, which does touch them, depends on E3 and will use «its current step»).
- D2 (2026-11-13, analyst, optional): steps are optional for any kind of task and any effort; a task without steps is created, shown and edited as today, and a task saved without steps has no steps (there is no migration). Because the plan asks not to require steps to create a task (E3, «Evitar»; rule 4) and it is the rule of `primerPaso` (D9 of `first-step`).
- D3 (2026-11-13, analyst, text of each step): each step follows the text rule of the first step (§4.1): a single line, with no line breaks and no maximum length, spaces at the start and at the end are trimmed on save, and an empty step or one with only spaces is not saved (it is discarded on save, without error). A step is never invalid: it never rejects the creation or the edit. Because it is the first step rule, of which the steps are the extension, and the plan asks not to put obstacles.
- D4 (2026-11-13, analyst, which rows show it): the step that the row shows follows the rule of D10 and D13 of `first-step` (§6.1): only in `pending` and `overdue` rows, decided with the status of each moment, never in `waiting` (postponed and completed ones included) or in paused ones; an `overdue` task with the checkbox disabled by its window shows it. It is shown in full, on several lines if needed, in secondary text without emphasis or its own color. Because it is the same line of the row that already exists and the user already chose that rule.
- D5 (2026-11-13, analyst, what is not shown): the steps and their progress are not shown in the Historial (§6.3), in the undo notice (§5.4), in Anotaciones (§6.7) or in the copied text. The row never shows the whole list. Because it is D7 of `first-step` and the plan asks not to show the whole breakdown.
- D6 (2026-11-13, analyst; reconciled with D9 and D11): postponing, removing the postponement, pausing, resuming and changing the kind or the mode do not erase or change the steps or the step the task is on. Because they are part of the definition of the task, like the note and the first step (D5 of `first-step`). Only completing and undoing move the progress (D11).
- D7 (2026-11-13, analyst, Unsaved changes): editing the steps in the panel counts for **Unsaved changes** with the single definition of §6.2: typing and going back to the original value does not count, nor do the spaces that **Guardar** trims or an empty step that **Guardar** discards; adding, removing or changing a step does. Because it is the single definition of «there are changes». Tapping «Hecho» or «Paso anterior» in the row does not go through the panel or through **Unsaved changes**.
- D8 (2026-11-13, user, relation with the first step): Q1 «Una sola lista (Recomendada)» (translation: «A single list (Recommended)»). The first step is step 1 and more can be added below it in the panel. The rule of §4.1 «There is no way to mark it as done and no progress indicator» stops applying to tasks with more than one step; one-step tasks keep it (D22).
- D9 (2026-11-13, user, how to advance): Q2 «Botón en la fila (Recomendada)» (translation: «Button in the row (Recommended)»). A button in the row, next to the step that is shown, marks it as done and the row shows the next one; the task stores which step it is on, also for the other device.
- D10 (2026-11-13, user, last step): Q3 «Avanzar completa la tarea» (translation: «Advancing completes the task»). Tapping «Hecho» on the last step completes the task, with its undo notice, like the checkbox.
- D11 (2026-11-13, user, progress on completing): Q4 «Vuelve al paso 1 (Recomendada)» (translation: «Returns to step 1 (Recommended)»). Progress is per round: completing the task returns it to step 1, so a recurring task that comes back starts from the beginning; undoing the completion (notice, Historial or checkbox) restores the progress it had.
- D12 (2026-11-13, user, label in the row): Q5 «“Primer paso: …” y luego “Paso: …” (Recomendada)» (translation: «“Primer paso: …” and then “Paso: …” (Recommended)»). Without number or total.
- D13 (2026-11-13, user, correcting a progress): Q6 «Botón «Paso anterior» en la fila» (translation: «“Paso anterior” button in the row»). The row has a «Paso anterior» button that returns to the previous step.
- D14 (2026-11-13, user, maximum): Q7 «Sin máximo (Recomendada)» (translation: «No maximum (Recommended)»). It never rejects a save; the row shows only one at a time.
- D15 (2026-11-13, user, button text): Q8 «“Hecho” (Recomendada)» (translation: «“Hecho” (Recommended)»).
- D16 (2026-11-13, analyst, «Hecho» on the last step is completing; reconciled with D22): in a task of two steps or more, tapping «Hecho» on the last step does exactly what tapping the checkbox of that uncompleted task does: it adds the same completion (§4.4), opens the same notice «Completaste «…»» with **Deshacer** (§5.4, with «Vuelve …» if it is recurring) and returns the progress to step 1 (D11). If the checkbox is disabled because it is outside its window (§5.5, §6.1), the «Hecho» of the last step is also disabled; that of the other steps is not, because it completes nothing. Because the user chose that advancing the last step completes the task «like the checkbox» (D10) and the rule for completing exists in a single place.
- D17 (2026-11-13, analyst, undo after «Hecho» on the last step): **Deshacer** (notice, Historial or checkbox) deletes that completion and the task returns to the status that corresponds and to the last step, the progress it had before completing it (D11). There is no undo notice for «Hecho» on a step that is not the last: it is corrected with «Paso anterior» (D13). Because D11 says that undoing restores the progress and D13 chose the button, not the notice, to correct.
- D18 (2026-11-13, analyst, when the buttons are shown; reconciled with D22): «Hecho» and «Paso anterior» are shown only in tasks of two steps or more (D22), and they go on the step line, so they are shown only when the line is shown (D4: `pending` and `overdue`). «Paso anterior» is not shown on step 1, because there is no previous step. They are small text buttons, without their own color, «Paso anterior» before «Hecho»; tapping the step text, outside the buttons, is still tapping the row. Because the step line already decides its visibility with the status and rule 5 of the plan asks for literal text.
- D19 (2026-11-13, analyst, progress as a position): the progress is the number of the step the task is on. «Hecho» takes it to the step after the one that is shown and «Paso anterior» to the one before the one that is shown; neither goes past the first or the last. Editing the steps in the panel does not change that number: if fewer steps remain than the number, the row shows the last one (and if only one remains, the row is that of a one-step task: «Primer paso: …» without buttons, D22). The panel does not show or choose the current step (the user chose to correct from the row, D13). Because the app changes nothing by itself (rule 3) and so each gesture moves a single step.
- D20 (2026-11-13, analyst, synchronization): the list of steps is a single field that is replaced whole (like `etiquetas` and `horario`, §7) and the progress is another independent field; in each one the last change wins. Two «Hecho» offline on the same step leave the task on the next step, not two further along. Because it is the per-field conflict rule of §7 and a gesture in the row must not lose what the other device edited in the panel.
- D21 (2026-11-13, analyst, how they are edited in the panel): the «Primer paso» field stays where it is (§6.2); below it, each more step is a one-line field with the label «Paso 2», «Paso 3»…, with a **Quitar** button each, and at the end an **Agregar paso** button that adds an empty field, like **Agregar horario**. Enter in those fields saves (On-screen keyboard, §6.2). The steps go in the written order, without reordering; on save, the empty ones are discarded and the ones below move up (if the first step is left empty and step 2 is not, step 2 becomes the first step). Because it reuses the pattern of the schedules of the same panel, and D3 already says that an empty step is discarded.
- D22 (2026-11-13, user, one-step task): Q9 «No, se ve como hoy (Recomendada)» (translation: «No, it looks as it does today (Recommended)»). With a single step the row looks as it does today, without «Hecho» or «Paso anterior», and the task is completed with the checkbox. The first step rule «There is no way to mark it as done» still holds for those tasks.

- **Reuses**:
  - `crearTarea` and `DatosComunes` (`creacion.ts`): `pasos` is a field common to every kind, like emoji, note and first step. `crearPeriodica` for calendar (`crear-periodica-calendario.ts`) is the only creator that does not propagate the common fields with a spread: `pasos` has to be passed to it by hand. `crearSegunDatos` and `datosDeTarea` (`datos-de-tarea.ts`) remain inverses.
  - `normalizarPrimerPaso` / `primer-paso.ts` and `camposDeTexto` (`campos-de-texto.ts`): each step follows the same text rule as the first step; the normalization of the list (trim, discard empty ones, move up, the first step left empty so step 2 moves up) lives in a new module `pasos.ts`, and that of the progress (next, previous, cap, which step is shown, which buttons) in `paso-actual.ts`, so as not to mix jobs.
  - `editar` (`edicion.ts`): `pasos` enters the change only if it changed and is replaced whole, and `pasoActual` is another field; changing the kind does not touch them.
  - `completar` (`completados.ts`): adds the `pasoAlCompletar` to the completion of a task of two steps or more, according to the `pasoActual` of the moment (with the cap). `accionDelCheckbox` and `checkboxHabilitado` (`checkbox.ts`): the «Hecho» of the last step is enabled the same as the checkbox; there is no other window rule.
  - `filasDePendientes` (`pendientes.ts`) and `clasificarEnPendientes` (`completada.ts`): the status of each row is already decided there; the step line and the buttons are shown according to that status, without storing anything or reordering.
  - `hayCambios` and the comparison keys (`cambios-sin-guardar.ts`): the single definition of "there are changes".
  - The deletion of a completion (`borrado.ts`, `historial.ts`, `aviso.ts`): the new core function that says which `pasoActual` to write when deleting a completion is used from the three paths (notice, Historial, checkbox); today the core does not have a single place that deletes a completion: `paso-actual.ts` adds it without duplicating the rule.
- **Notes**:
  - The exported signatures that `acceptance/` and the UI already use stay compatible (only new optional fields and parameters). `nombreDeTarea` (`nombre-de-tarea.ts`) does not change: the name is still emoji and title.
  - A completion of a task of two steps or more without `pasoAlCompletar` (this does not happen in new data) does not change `pasoActual` when deleted.
  - Edge cases that cannot be written as Gherkin and go to unit and property tests of the core: `pasoActual` greater than the number of steps (the last one is used) in each operation (show, «Hecho», «Paso anterior», store `pasoAlCompletar`), `pasoAlCompletar` greater than the steps that remain on undo (cap), and step 0 or negative if some old data brought it.
- **Gate**: `node scripts/gate.ts core`
