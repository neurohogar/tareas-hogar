# Package: guilt-free-status

First feature of epic E2 of `docs/v3/plan.md` («Estado sin culpa y reglas a la vista» (translation: «Guilt-free status and rules in view»)): the row of an overdue task says when it was due, as a fact with a date and a distance («Tocaba el martes 10 · hace 3 días») and without an alarm color; waiting tasks say when they come back («Vuelve el jueves 19 · en 6 días»), and on completing, the notice says the next date. Only texts and color: the status rules (§5) and the order of the list (§6.1) do not change.

## Specs

- **S1** (§6.1): «If the task is `overdue`, the overdue text «Tocaba …» (**Date and distance**, below), which replaces «Atrasada» as the overdue indicator. It carries no alarm color: it is shown in the same color and the same secondary text size as the effort and the schedule, without red or emphasis. It is not shown in a paused task.»
- **S2** (§6.1): «In `waiting` tasks: dimmed style, a thin progress bar with the time until they come back and the return text «Vuelve …» (**Date and distance**, below). It also applies to the completed recurring tasks that are shown with **Mostrar completadas** and to the postponed ones, without naming the postponement in the row.»
- **S3** (§6.1): «`pending` rows, the paused ones and the completed one-off tasks do not show date or distance text.»
- **S4** (§6.1): «**Phrase**: «Tocaba <fecha>[ · <distancia>]» in an `overdue` task and «Vuelve <fecha>[ · <distancia>]» in a `waiting` one. «el» goes only before the date with a day of the week; with «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana» it does not. Examples (now = Friday 13 November 2026, 10:00): «Tocaba el martes 10 · hace 3 días», «Tocaba ayer», «Tocaba hoy 09:00 · hace 1 hora», «Tocaba hoy 10:00 · ahora», «Vuelve mañana», «Vuelve hoy 18:00 · en 8 horas», «Vuelve hoy 10:40 · en 40 minutos», «Vuelve el jueves 19 · en 6 días». No congratulations or other words.»
- **S5** (§6.1): «**Instant of «Tocaba»** (since when the task is due; it is never a future instant):»
- **S6** (§6.1): «If `A` has not arrived yet (the task became overdue by the overdue time without a schedule of §5.5 before its window opened), the instant without a schedule is used: `V`, or 00:00 of `N`. A one-off task without a due date is never `overdue`, so it carries no «Tocaba».»
- **S7** (§6.1): «**Instant of «Vuelve»**: the same one with which this section sorts the `waiting` tasks (when it becomes pending again): relative, `V`; calendar, 00:00 of `N`; with a schedule, `A` (§5.5; a one-off task outside a window, the `desde` of the next one); postponed, `pospuesta.hasta` (§5.9). If two causes have it `waiting`, the instant at which it stops being so (the later of the two).»
- **S8** (§6.1): «**Date**: «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana» if the instant falls on those local days (the day starts at 00:00); if not, the day of the week and the number («martes 10»), with « de <mes>» in lowercase if the month is not the current one («martes 28 de octubre») and « de <año>» if the year is not the current one («miércoles 31 de diciembre de 2025»). It carries the time `HH:MM` in 24 h (fixed-width digits) when the instant is not 00:00 (schedule, relative tasks, postponement): «hoy 18:00», «martes 10 19:30». It is the format of **Última vez** (§6.2) and of the **Historial** (§6.3).»
- **S9** (§6.1): «At 2 or more local days: «hace N días» / «en N días» (difference in local days, like **Última vez**), with or without a time in the date.»
- **S10** (§6.1): «With the date «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana» and with a time: in hours («hace 3 horas», «en 1 hora») or, at less than an hour, in minutes («hace 40 minutos», «en 40 minutos»). The past is rounded down and the future up, never «en 0 minutos». It is counted from now, even if it crosses midnight («Tocaba ayer 23:00 · hace 2 horas» at 01:00).»
- **S11** (§6.1): «With the date «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana» and without a time: there is no distance, the word already is one («Tocaba ayer», «Vuelve mañana»).»
- **S12** (§6.1): «**It updates by itself** as time passes (at midnight, at the hour or at the minute that applies), like **Última vez** with the panel open. It does not change the status or the order of the list.»
- **S13** (§6.1): «They are only texts and color: the statuses of §5 and the order of this section do not change.»
- **S14** (§5.4): «If the completed task comes back (recurring), below «Completaste …» the notice says when: «Vuelve el jueves 19 · en 6 días», with the return instant computed after the completion and the format of **Date and distance** (§6.1), the same text its `waiting` row will show. A one-off task (which does not come back) shows only «Completaste …». There is no other closing and no congratulations.»
- **S15** (§6.1): «**Mostrar pausadas**: a check next to "Completadas", with the text "Pausadas". Off (the initial value), the paused tasks (§5.8) are not shown. On, they go at the end of the list (after the completed ones, if those are shown), by title in the alphabetical order above; each row dimmed, with the text "Pausada" instead of the overdue or return text and the checkbox disabled. The label filter also applies to the paused ones. The value is saved on that device, like "Mostrar completadas".»
- **S16** (§6.1): «With the date «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana» and with a time, if the instant falls less than a minute in the past, the distance is «ahora», not «hace 0 minutos» («Tocaba hoy 10:00 · ahora» at 10:00:30). The future still rounds up: never «en 0 minutos».»

## Decisions

- D1 (2026-11-13, analyst, scope): this feature covers three of the four proposals of E2: the overdue status as a fact with a date and without an alarm color, the relative distance next to the exact date (overdue and return of waiting tasks) and the informative closing on completing. The «por qué está aquí» (translation: «why it is here») that can be consulted per task is left for a separate feature of the same epic (`reglas-a-la-vista`). Because the plan has the analyst split each epic into features, and the «why it is here» needs its own decisions (where it is consulted, which rule explains it) that do not depend on these.
- D2 (2026-11-13, analyst): the status rules of §5 do not change: which task is `pendiente`, `atrasada` or `en espera`, when it goes from one to another and what can be completed stay the same. The order of the list of §6.1 does not change either (D11). Because the plan puts it under «Evitar» (translation: «Avoid») («esta épica toca solo la presentación» (translation: «this epic touches only the presentation»)).
- D3 (2026-11-13, analyst): the overdue text of the row (the «Tocaba …» of D12, which replaces «Atrasada» as the overdue indicator of §6.1) loses the reddish color (`--atraso`) and is shown in the same color and secondary text size as the effort and the schedule. Because the plan asks for «sin color de alarma» (translation: «no alarm color») and forbids «muros rojos» (translation: «red walls») (global rule 2).
- D4 (2026-11-13, analyst): the row of a `waiting` task shows, besides the progress bar (which stays), a text with when it comes back: «Vuelve el jueves 19 · en 6 días». The instant is the same one with which §6.1 sorts the `waiting` tasks («cuándo vuelve a pendiente» (translation: «when it becomes pending again»), with §5.5 and §5.9: with a schedule, `A`; postponed, `pospuesta.hasta`); if two causes have it waiting, the instant at which it stops being so. It also applies to the completed recurring tasks that are shown with **Mostrar completadas** (they are `waiting`). Because the plan asks for the relative distance «también para el regreso de las tareas en espera» (translation: «also for the return of waiting tasks»), and a single instant prevents the text from contradicting the order.
- D5 (2026-11-13, analyst): a postponed task shows the same «Vuelve …» as any `waiting` task, without naming the postponement in the row; the panel still shows «Pospuesta hasta HH:MM» as today. Because the row says when it comes back, and the cause is a matter of the «why it is here» (D1).
- D6 (2026-11-13, analyst): `pending` rows, the paused ones and the completed one-off tasks do not show new text. Because the plan only asks for a date for the overdue status and the return, and the row gains density (risk noted in E3 and E9).
- D7 (2026-11-13, analyst, exact date): the date is written «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana» if it falls on those local days; if not, day of the week and number («martes 10»), with « de <mes>» if the month is not the current one («martes 28 de octubre») and « de <año>» if the year is not the current one. It carries the time `HH:MM` in 24 h (fixed-width digits, §6) when the instant is not 00:00 (schedule, relative tasks, postponement): «hoy 18:00», «martes 10 19:30». Because it follows the example of the plan («martes 10») and the conventions of **Última vez** (§6.2) and of the Historial (§6.3).
- D8 (2026-11-13, analyst, relative distance): it goes after the date, separated by « · ». At 2 or more local days: «hace N días» / «en N días» (difference in local days, like **Última vez**). With the date written as «hoy», «ayer» or «mañana»: if it carries a time, in hours («hace 3 horas», «en 1 hora») or, at less than an hour, in minutes («en 40 minutos»; the past rounds down and the future up, never «en 0 minutos»); without a time, there is no distance because the word already is one. Because the plan asks for the distance next to the exact date and these are the units that the app already uses.
- D9 (2026-11-13, analyst): the date and distance texts update by themselves as time passes (at midnight, at the hour or at the minute that applies), like **Última vez** with the panel open. Because they derive from now and from visible rules; it is not a change of status or order that the app makes by itself (global rule 3).
- D10 (2026-11-13, analyst, closing on completing): the closing is the undo notice of §5.4, not a new notice: below «Completaste «🌱 Regar»» it adds «Vuelve el jueves 19 · en 6 días», with the instant of D4 computed after the completion and the format of D7, D8 and D14 (the same text its `waiting` row will show). No congratulations or other words. A one-off task (which does not come back) shows only «Completaste …», as today. Duration, Deshacer and replacement do not change. Because §5.4 is already the completion notice and there is only one at a time; the plan asks for a «breve» (translation: «brief») and «informativo» (translation: «informative») closing and puts congratulations under «Evitar».
- D11 (2026-11-13, user, order): P1 «No, §6.1 queda igual (Recomendada)» (translation: «No, §6.1 stays the same (Recommended)»). The order of the list does not change; the epic touches only texts and color.
- D12 (2026-11-13, user, text of the overdue row): P2 ««Tocaba el martes 10 · hace 3 días» (Recomendada)» (translation: ««Tocaba el martes 10 · hace 3 días» (Recommended)»), with the date on which it became due (due date or N; in a relative task, V; with a schedule, A) and the relative distance, without the word «Atrasada».
- D13 (2026-11-13, analyst, which date «Tocaba» shows): the instant of D12 by kind. One-off with a due date: the day `fechaLimite`, without a time, with or without a schedule (with a schedule, `A` of a one-off task is the first window after `creadaEn`, which can be weeks before the due date). Relative: `V` (with a time, D7). Calendar: the day of `N`, without a time. With a schedule, the recurring tasks use `A` (with several schedules, the start of `N`, §5.6 and §5.7). If `A` has not arrived yet (the task became overdue by the overdue time without a schedule of §5.5 before its window opened), the instant without a schedule is used (`V`, or 00:00 of `N`), so «Tocaba» never names a future instant. Because D12 chooses «la fecha límite» (translation: «the due date») for one-off tasks, and a «Tocaba» with a future date contradicts the word.
- D14 (2026-11-13, analyst, building the phrase): «Tocaba» and «Vuelve» carry «el» only before the form with a day of the week: «Tocaba el martes 10 · hace 3 días», «Tocaba ayer», «Tocaba hoy 09:00 · hace 3 horas», «Vuelve mañana», «Vuelve hoy 18:00 · en 40 minutos», «Vuelve el jueves 19 · en 6 días». The distance follows D8 in both phrases. Because «el hoy» or «el mañana» are not Spanish, and so a single rule builds the date for the overdue row, the waiting row and the notice of D10.
- D15 (2026-11-13, user, past of less than a minute): «hace 0 minutos tiene q ser ahora» (translation: ««hace 0 minutos» has to be «ahora»»). When the instant of «Tocaba» (hoy, ayer or mañana with a time) falls less than a minute in the past, the distance is «ahora» instead of being omitted or saying «hace 0 minutos»: «Tocaba hoy 10:00 · ahora». The future still rounds up, never «en 0 minutos». It changes the distance rule of §6.1.

- **Reuses**:
  - `clasificarEnPendientes` (`completada.ts`) and `estadoDeTarea`/`esperaPorPosposicion` (`estado-de-tarea.ts`) are still the only entry point of the status: the text is derived from the status they already give, it does not recompute it (S13).
  - `instanteDeUrgencia` (`urgencia.ts`) is the instant with which a `waiting` task is sorted (S7): the «Vuelve …» uses the same one, except with two causes (the later of the two, `hasta` of the postponement against V or A). That of «Tocaba» (S5) is another: for an overdue task, `instanteDeUrgencia` gives since when it is overdue (the delay, e.g. C or V + interval), not since when it is due; A, V, the day of N or the day of `fechaLimite` is needed.
  - `plazosConHorario` (`plazos-con-horario.ts`) and `estado-con-horario.ts` give A, C and the overdue time with a schedule (one and several windows); `proximaOcurrencia` (`estado-periodica-calendario.ts`) gives N; `instanteDeUrgenciaRelativa` (`estado-periodica-relativa.ts`) gives V; `comoRecienCreada` (`pausa.ts`) for a resumed task.
  - `diasEntre`, `inicioDelDia`, `sumarDias`, `diaDeLaSemana`, `diaDelMes`, `mesDe`, `anioDe` (`dias.ts`), `horaEnTexto` and the month table of `fechas-en-texto.ts`, and `ultimaVez` (`ultima-vez.ts`) as the model of the format; the texts are a function of `ahora`, which the core receives as a parameter.
  - `avisoAlCompletar` and `avisoVisible` (`aviso.ts`): the return of the notice is computed with the task and the completions after the completion.
- **Notes**: the exported signatures that `acceptance/` and the UI already use (`filasDePendientes`, `avisoAlCompletar`, `avisoVisible`, `instanteDeUrgencia`, `estadoDeTarea`) stay compatible: only new optional fields or parameters, because the core gate runs the already implemented acceptance and the order does not change (S13). Ambiguities of the spec that the scenarios avoid on purpose: a different year with the same month as now (S8 says « de <mes>» only if the month is not the current one; in all the rows, when the year changes the month changes too), and in calendar the instant without a schedule «00:00 de N» of S6 is not reached (with a schedule, A ≤ C).
