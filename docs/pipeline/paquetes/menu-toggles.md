# Package: menu-toggles

The **Completadas** and **Pausadas** checks leave the top of **Pendientes** and become on/off items of the **⋮** menu, with the same texts, in the order Modo mínimo, Completadas, Pausadas, Reprogramar vencidas, and with no indicator outside the menu. A completed one-off task is never shown in **Pendientes** (with **Mostrar completadas** on or off): **Mostrar completadas** shows only the completed recurring tasks, and after the undo notice closes the **Historial** is the only way to open or un-complete a completed one-off task.

## Specs

- **S1** (§5.1): «| Has at least one completion | Completed: it leaves the list and is shown in the history. It is never shown in **Pendientes**, with **Mostrar completadas** (§6.1) on or off. |»
- **S2** (§5.4): «A completion of the minimal version is deleted like any other (notice, **Historial** or, for a recurring task, the checkbox of the completed task) and the status is recomputed with those that remain. The checkbox of a completed recurring task deletes the most recent completion, whether whole or minimal; that of a recurring task completed on an earlier day completes it again, as a whole.»
- **S3** (§5.5): «A recurring task `waiting` because of its schedule that has completions is completed (§6.1): with **Mostrar completadas** off it appears only when the window opens.»
- **S4** (§5.5): «**Completing** outside the window is not possible: the checkbox that would complete is disabled (§6.1), and so is «Hice la mínima» (§6.1). Deleting a completion (checkbox of a completed recurring task, notice, **Historial**) is possible at any hour.»
- **S5** (§6.1): «**Menu and minimal mode**: the header of **Pendientes** has a small **⋮** button, with the accessible name «Menú», that opens a menu with four items, in this order: **Modo mínimo**, "Completadas" (**Mostrar completadas**, below), "Pausadas" (**Mostrar pausadas**, below) and **Reprogramar vencidas** (below). **Modo mínimo**, "Completadas" and "Pausadas" are on/off items: each one shows a check mark when on, and tapping it acts at that moment, without confirmation: it turns its value on or off (§5.8 for the mode) and closes the menu. The menu has no other items. The three on/off items are always enabled, also with the mode on and with nothing to show. Nothing outside the menu shows the value of "Completadas" or "Pausadas" (there is no indicator in **Pendientes**), except the rows themselves. While the mode is on, above the list there is a line with the text «Modo mínimo» in secondary text, without color, icon or counter. It is only text (tapping it does nothing) and it is shown even if the list is empty (with the mode on and no essential task, the list is empty with that line). Turning the mode on or off does not show any other message (§5.8).»
- **S6** (§6.1): «**Reprogramar vencidas** (§5.11): the last item of the **⋮** menu, below "Pausadas". It is **disabled** (shown dimmed, tapping it does nothing and the menu stays open) whenever there is no overdue recurring task in view, including when the only overdue tasks are one-off ones or, with the minimal mode on, non-essential ones; the menu always looks the same. Enabled, tapping it closes the menu and opens a **preview**: a title «Reprogramar vencidas», a line «Vuelven como recién creadas, desde ahora.», the list of the tasks that will be rescheduled, and the buttons **Reprogramar** and **Cancelar**. Each row shows only the emoji and the title (§4.1), in the order of this section, with no «Tocaba …», no date, no checks and no count. The preview follows the data and the clock while it is open: a task that another device completed, deleted or paused, or that stops being an overdue recurring task, leaves it, and one that becomes one enters it. **Reprogramar** acts on all the tasks that are in the preview at that moment, without further confirmation, closes the preview and shows the notice of §5.4. If the preview is left with no task, **Reprogramar** is disabled and it can only be closed; **Cancelar** closes it and changes nothing.»
- **S7** (§6.1): «**Mostrar pausadas**: the item "Pausadas" of the **⋮** menu (above), an on/off item. Off (the initial value), the paused tasks (§5.8) are not shown. With the mode on, it shows only the tasks paused by hand that are essential (the ones paused by the mode are never shown, above). On, they go at the end of the list (after the completed ones, if those are shown), by title in the alphabetical order above; each row dimmed, with the text "Pausada" instead of the overdue or return text and the checkbox disabled. The value is saved on that device, like **Mostrar completadas**: a device that already had it on keeps it on.»
- **S8** (§6.1): «**Mostrar completadas**: the item "Completadas" of the **⋮** menu (above), an on/off item.»
- **S9** (§6.1): «A task is **completed** if it is one-off with at least one completion (§5.1; it is never shown in **Pendientes**, below), or recurring `waiting` with at least one completion (§5.2, §5.3). A recurring `waiting` task without completions (calendar, before its first occurrence) is not completed.»
- **S10** (§6.1): «On: it adds only the completed **recurring** tasks; the completed one-off tasks are never shown in **Pendientes**, with it on or off (after the undo notice of §5.4 closes, the **Historial**, §6.3, is the only way to open or un-complete one). After the uncompleted ones (in the order above) come the completed recurring ones, from the one with the most recent `completadoEn` to the oldest; if they tie, the same tie-breaks as above apply.»
- **S11** (§6.1): «The value is saved **on that device** (it is not synced, it is not in Firestore) and survives reloading the app; a device that already had it on keeps it on.»
- **S12** (§6.1): «**Contrast**: the title of an uncompleted task is shown in **bold**; that of a completed (recurring) one, struck through, in normal weight and dimmed, with the checkbox checked. The uncompleted ones stand out from the completed ones at a glance.»
- **S13** (§6.1): «`pending` rows and the paused ones do not show date or distance text.»
- **S14** (§6.1): «Tapping the checkbox of an uncompleted task completes it. Tapping the checkbox of a completed task (always recurring: a completed one-off task has no row in **Pendientes**):»
- **S15** (§6.1): «Whose most recent completion is from today or later (local day of `completadoEn`; later only with clocks out of sync, §5.2): it deletes it (like **Deshacer**, §5.4) and the task returns to the status that applies.»
- **S16** (§6.2): «A task with completions that becomes calendar takes `P` according to §5.3 (the completions without `ocurrenciaCubierta` contribute the day of their `completadoEn`).»
- **S17** (§6.2): «A completed one-off task has no row in **Pendientes**, with **Mostrar completadas** on or off (§6.1); if it is edited (for example, from the history) and becomes recurring, it is governed by the recurring rules with those completions.»
- **S18** (§6.3): «It does not show the orphan completions (§7) or those of deleted tasks (§6.2).»

## Decisions

- D1 (2026-11-23, analyst): the two new menu items behave like **Modo mínimo**: each one shows a check mark when on, tapping it acts at that moment without confirmation, turns the value on or off and closes the menu. Because **Modo mínimo** is already a switch item of the same menu and §6.1 has no other switch pattern.
- D2 (2026-11-23, analyst): both values stay preferences **of that device** (not synced, not in Firestore), survive reloading the app and keep the value already saved on each device (whoever had «Completadas» on still has it on after the change), with off as the initial value. Because the request moves where they are tapped, not what they are (§6.1 «The value is saved on that device»).
- D3 (2026-11-23, analyst): the two items are always enabled, also with the minimal mode on and with nothing to show; the menu always looks the same. Because §6.1 already demands «the menu always looks the same» for **Reprogramar vencidas** and the effect of the checks with the mode on is already defined (Which rows are in view with the mode on).
- D4 (2026-11-23, analyst): with **Mostrar completadas** on, the completed recurring tasks keep their current rule (order by most recent `completadoEn`, struck through, «Vuelve …», after the uncompleted ones and before the paused ones). Because the request only removes the one-off ones.
- D5 (2026-11-23, analyst): the undo notice of §5.4 (with **Deshacer**) and the **Historial** (§6.3: entries, **Borrar** with its confirmation, opening the panel) keep their behaviour for a completed one-off task; after the notice closes they are the only way to reach it: tapping its entry opens **Editar tarea**, and **Borrar** is the only way to un-complete it. Because D7 removes its row (and with it its checkbox) from **Pendientes**, and the user accepted the Historial as the only way in D7.
- D6 (2026-11-23, analyst): a recurring task with completions that becomes one-off on editing (§6.2) is a completed one-off task, so on saving it leaves **Pendientes** with **Mostrar completadas** on or off and is reached only from the **Historial**. Because D7 applies to every completed one-off task, whatever its origin.

- D7 (2026-11-23, user, Q1 completed one-off tasks): «Nunca se muestra en Pendientes (Recomendada)» (translation: «It is never shown in Pendientes (Recommended)»). Option description: neither with the option on nor off; after the Deshacer notice closes, the only way to open it or un-complete it is the Historial (tap the entry, or Borrar); it can no longer be un-completed by tapping its checkbox in the list.
- D8 (2026-11-23, user, Q2 order in the ⋮ menu): «Junto a Modo mínimo» (translation: «Next to Modo mínimo»). Option description: Modo mínimo, Completadas, Pausadas, Reprogramar vencidas; the three on/off items sit together and Reprogramar vencidas moves from second to last.
- D9 (2026-11-23, user, Q3 menu texts): ««Completadas» y «Pausadas»» (translation: «"Completadas" and "Pausadas"»). Option description: the same words as today; a bare «Completadas» in a menu can be read as an action or as a screen.
- D10 (2026-11-23, user, Q4 indicator outside the menu): «No, solo el menú (Recomendada)» (translation: «No, only the menu (Recommended)»). Option description: the menu shows it with its check mark; the list stays as clean as today; the completed rows (struck through) and paused rows («Pausada») already show what they are; the user only finds out by opening the menu.

- D11 (2026-11-23, analyst): the definition of **completed** of §6.1 does not change (a one-off task with at least one completion is still completed: it is not in view with **Mostrar completadas** off, and the status rules of §5 do not change); D7 only removes it from the rows that **Mostrar completadas** on adds. Because the request filters which completed rows are shown, not what a completed task is.
- D12 (2026-11-23, analyst): the rules about a completed one-off row in **Pendientes** (tapping its checkbox deletes its most recent completion, its row has no date text, it goes struck through at the end) are removed from the spec, not kept as unreachable rules; the checkbox of a completed recurring task keeps its rules (§5.4, §5.5, §6.1). Because with D7 that row no longer exists and the spec only describes what can happen.
- D13 (2026-11-23, analyst): the Completadas and Pausadas items keep the internal names **Mostrar completadas** and **Mostrar pausadas** in the spec; only their place and their row in the menu change. Because D9 keeps the texts shown, and the rules of §5 and §6 already refer to them by those names.
