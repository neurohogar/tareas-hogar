# Package: remove-annotations

Annotations leave the project. There is no **Anotar** button or box anywhere, no Anotaciones screen or tab, no «Anotación guardada» confirmation, no **Copiar** with its «Anotaciones copiadas» / «No se pudo copiar» texts, and deleting a task no longer touches annotations. The tab bar keeps **Pendientes** and **Historial**; `/anotaciones` opens Pendientes like `/etiquetas` does. Nothing replaces them. Deleting the stored annotations in production is a separate, later step, not part of this feature (D3).

## Decisions

- D1 (2026-11-20, user, via orchestrator): «la anotaciones no sirvieron para nada, me da paja usar eso y es muy ambiguo» → answer to the orchestrator's question: «Sacarlas del proyecto». The annotations concept (the `/anotaciones` screen, the «Anotar» button and box, the annotation core modules, adapters, features, QA procedures, spec sections and e2e scripts) leaves the project, as labels and effort did.
- D2 (2026-11-20, analyst, what disappears): everything that exists only for annotations goes, with no replacement: the glossary row **Anotación** (§3); the §2 bullet «Anotaciones «me sirvió / me molestó» …»; the Anotación entity with its table and rules (§4.5); the whole of §5.10 Anotar (button, two taps, context, «no toca la tarea ni el panel», closing without annotating, the «Anotación guardada» confirmation, «nunca se pide», deleting a task deletes its annotations); the whole of §6.7 Anotaciones (list, grouping, Editar texto, Borrar, **Copiar** with «Anotaciones copiadas» and «No se pudo copiar»). The section headings stay as «(eliminada)» with the sentence of §4.3 and §6.4, so §5.10's and §6.7's numbers and every other reference stay stable. Because D1 removes the concept entirely and none of these parts has a meaning without it. **Copiar** and its clipboard-rejection notice exist only for annotations (no other screen copies anything), so they go with them; the undo notice (§5.4) does not change.
- D3 (2026-11-20, analyst, the stored data; orchestrator default D-a): from this feature's deploy on, the `users/{uid}/anotaciones` collection may still exist. The app does not read, show, write or delete it: **Borrar tarea** deletes the task and its completions in one `writeBatch` and no longer deletes that task's annotations, and there is no cleanup of orphan annotations. Whether and when the production annotations are deleted is a separate script that the orchestrator runs only with the user's explicit confirmation of that exact script; it is NOT part of this feature's code, and the app must behave the same before and after it. Because only a deliberate migration may delete user data (precedent D2/D15 of `remove-labels-and-effort`), and leaving annotations of deleted tasks in a collection nobody reads has no visible effect.
- D4 (2026-11-20, analyst, navigation; orchestrator default D-b): the tab bar has two tabs, in this order: **Pendientes** (`/`) and **Historial** (`/historial`), and the current one is shown selected; «sin contador ni marca de anotaciones nuevas» goes. Opening `/anotaciones` (a bookmark, the browser history, or reloading a tab that was there when the new version arrived) opens Pendientes, replacing that history entry, with no notice, exactly like `/etiquetas` (§6 **Navegación**, S14 of `remove-labels-and-effort`); the two old routes are described in one sentence. Because a two-tab bar is what removing one tab of three leaves, and changing the navigation model (a single Historial button, no bar) would be a new feature, not a removal; the redirect keeps **Atrás** from landing on an empty page (§1, less visual noise).
- D5 (2026-11-20, analyst, rules that only mention annotations): they lose the mention and keep everything else. §4.1 **Primer paso**: «No se ve en el Historial, en el aviso de deshacer, en Anotaciones ni en el texto copiado» becomes «No se ve en el Historial ni en el aviso de deshacer». §4.1 **Pasos**, **Sin otros efectos**: «no se ven en el Historial, en el aviso de deshacer, en Anotaciones ni en el texto copiado (la palabra «Mínima» …)» becomes «no se ven en el Historial ni en el aviso de deshacer (la palabra «Mínima» …)». §4.1 **Versión mínima**: «No se ve en Anotaciones ni en el texto copiado; el Historial y el aviso de deshacer solo muestran la palabra «Mínima», nunca su texto» becomes «El Historial y el aviso de deshacer solo muestran la palabra «Mínima», nunca su texto». §6.2 **Atrás**: the item «el recuadro de **Anotar** (§5.10) se cierra sin anotar» and the sentence «Con el panel cerrado y el recuadro de **Anotar** abierto, **Atrás** lo cierra y no sale de la pantalla donde está» go. §6.2 **Borrar tarea**: «y **todas sus anotaciones** (y solo las suyas, §5.10), que desaparecen de la pantalla Anotaciones» goes. §6 **Alcance** of the bundled font and emoji and of **Orientación**: the screen lists lose «Anotaciones». ## Open questions

- P1 (plan only; the app spec does not depend on it): `docs/v3/plan.md` uses annotations as the validation instrument of every epic («Cómo se valida cada épica», and the «Señal: notas …» of E2–E12). Without annotations, how is an epic judged (keep, change or retire)?
  - a) **By the user's own account after four weeks of use, told in conversation, plus the data the app already stores (Recommended)**. Consequence: the four-week rule and «Qué se decide» stay; «Cómo se lee» and each «Señal: notas …» become «what the user reports» or the stored data (first steps filled, minimal completions, etc.); no new app feature.
  - b) **Only the data the app already stores, read by hand**. Consequence: each epic's signal is rewritten as a count or a field in the account; epics whose signal was only a note (E2, E6, E8, E11, E12) need a new measurable signal or have none.
  - c) **Drop the validation section from the plan**. Consequence: epics are kept or retired ad hoc; the «Hipótesis y validación» lines lose their «Señal» and keep only the hypothesis.
